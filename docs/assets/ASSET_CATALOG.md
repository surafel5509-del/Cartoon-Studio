# Asset Catalog

The library is designed as a large production catalog.

## Catalog Groups

### Characters
30+ initial character packages with distinct silhouettes, styles, roles and animation sets.

### Props
Furniture, tools, toys, weapons where appropriate to fictional content, food, books, containers, sports objects, technology and household objects.

### Environments
Rooms, streets, schools, parks, forests, cities, villages, interiors and cinematic locations.

### Nature
Grass, flowers, trees, rocks, soil, leaves, bushes, clouds, rain and environmental elements.

### VFX
Smoke, dust, sparks, magic, impact effects, fire-like stylized effects and motion accents.

### Textures
Paper, fabric, wood, stone, metal, ground, walls and stylized hand-painted textures.

### Audio
Footsteps, impacts, ambience, nature, UI sounds, voices and reusable sound cues.

The catalog architecture is scalable and should support content packs without changing engine code.
